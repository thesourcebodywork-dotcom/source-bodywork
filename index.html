MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Care rooted in curiosity and connection.

Source Bodywork is an independently owned practice built on a simple belief: meaningful therapeutic care starts with being heard.

At Source Bodywork, I believe you are already the brightest and most beautiful version of yourself. Sometimes, it's simply a matter of removing the things that keep you from seeing and feeling that for yourself. Tension, stress, and the patterns we carry in our bodies can create distance between us and our sense of self. My intention is to help you find your way back. I'm a licensed massage therapist, but I'm also a client. Growing up, I participated in just about every sport and activity imaginable, collecting aches and pains like honorary badges and constantly searching for ways to find relief. When I began studying at the Columbus School of Medical Massage, so much of what I'd been curious about my entire life finally started to click. My goal is to be the massage therapist who helps you recognize real progress in your body. Someone who listens, works alongside you, and celebrates the moments when something that once felt painful or restricted begins to feel easier. I believe massage therapy is collaborative, and I approach every session that way. By combining my knowledge of the body with careful listening, intentional release techniques, and breathwork, I work with your body to address areas of tension and long-standing holding patterns. I want to understand not only where you're experiencing discomfort, but what may be contributing to it. Every session begins with meeting you where you are and working together toward where you'd like to be.
