THE SOURCE PHILOSOPHY

Your body
has a story.

Every experience we move through leaves an impression. Sometimes it settles into our bodies as tension, discomfort, or patterns we've carried for so long that we no longer recognize them.

At Source, I believe bodywork is an invitation to listen more closely. To soften what we've been holding, reconnect with the wisdom of our bodies, and create space for the parts of ourselves that have been waiting to emerge.

My hope is not to change who you are, but to help you find your way back to the essence of who you've always been.

Here, your body is never something to fix. It's something worth understanding.
